# Clanker University — Huseyin's answers to the 55-decision docket
Answered 2026-08-31. All 55 closed. Source: DOCKET-FINAL.md.

## The shape these answers describe

Not one of the six bundles. It is closest to **Commons**, but with full course
generation and user-supplied textbooks — a configuration the docket priced but never
assembled:

- Free, open-source **desktop app**, distributed like opencode. User brings their own
  inference API key. No hosted service, no accounts, no payments, no entity.
- The app **generates everything** — curriculum, lectures, coursework, tests — locally,
  on the user's machine, with the user's models and the user's materials.
- User may supply a PDF textbook to ground a course.
- **Any subject, any length.** Single course through four-year equivalent. Scope is
  settled in conversation with the counselor. No exclusion list.
- No credential, no transcript, no artifact. No integrity claims, no efficacy claims.
- State lives locally, with loud export/import.
- Test-out: take the final for any course you claim to know; pass and it is marked complete.

## Deferred monetization (not built now)

1. Referral agreement with a multi-provider inference service (OpenRouter-like), placed
   as the "recommended" option for less technical users, for a commission.
2. Pre-made courses, generated once by Huseyin from permissively licensed material and
   sold below what it would cost a user to generate — a different legal regime from the
   free tool, and one that reactivates the entity, tax, claims and corpus questions.

There is no incentive to cheat since there is no diploma that stands as proof on its own.
